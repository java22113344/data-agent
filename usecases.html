Finance & FP&A

Close the month without waiting on the data team. Reconcile, variance-analyze, and explain the numbers in one conversation.

  • Ask “what drove the margin drop?” and get the query that answered it
  • Charts exported straight to Power BI as a real .pbix
  • Ledger data stays in a warehouse file on your own disk
Marketing & Growth

Campaign performance, funnels, and cohort cuts — without piping customer data through a third-party BI cloud.

  • Blend ad-spend CSVs with product events in minutes
  • Find the channel that actually pays back
  • Share dashboards and exports, not raw PII
Operations & Supply Chain

Inventory, fulfilment, and SLA monitoring over warehouse tables — with questions you can ask in plain English.

  • Find the SKU that is always out of stock
  • Join logistics exports without a pipeline project
  • Works with the network unplugged
Healthcare & Life Sciences

De-identified operational analytics where compliance says the data cannot leave the building.

  • Run a local model and no PHI reaches an external API
  • Parameterized SQL only; inputs validated at the boundary
  • Audit-friendly: every query is recorded
Education & Research

Teach SQL with an assistant that shows its work — or explore study data without a grant for cloud compute.

  • Students can read the query behind every answer
  • The free tier is enough to try the whole workflow
  • Runs on a laptop; no cluster required
Public Sector & Regulated Teams

Procurement-friendly architecture: open source, self-hostable, inspectable end to end.

  • No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting from the app — the warehouse stays on the machine unless you configure a cloud model
  • The app checks GitHub for updates, and two paid features route traffic externally — see the full network disclosure
  • Install from a single Windows .exe
  • Read the whole stack before you approve it
