Billing

Choose your plan

One price list, in rupees: Free is ₹0, Pro is ₹499/mo, and Lifetime is ₹10,000 once. The free tier gives you 25 AI requests a day, 3 saved dashboards and 7 days of chat history — no card, no checkout. Pro auto-renews monthly (cancel anytime), raises the allowance to 50 AI requests a day, and adds the export, automation, live-connection and governance tools; Lifetime gives you 100 AI requests a day and no further payment. Checkout is completed securely on Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking) and attaches to the email you pay with, so there are no codes to paste.

Still deciding? The FAQ answers the usual billing questions.
